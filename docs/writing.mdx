## Why I Write

Technical writing is how I consolidate my understanding. Explaining a system clearly enough for another engineer to follow forces precision: edge cases surface, assumptions become explicit, and architectural weaknesses become visible before they become bugs in production.

I also write because the developer community runs on shared knowledge. The articles, tutorials, and documentation that helped me get started are the kind of resources I want to contribute back. My writing spans AI system architecture, cybersecurity implementation, and software engineering, with a focus on transparent, practical explanations.
