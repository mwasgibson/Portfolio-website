I am a Software Engineering student at Kisii University. I began my degree in 2022 and expect to complete it in 2027. My interests sit across software engineering, applied artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, and technical research.

I learn by building. My projects have taken me from web applications and backend APIs into machine-learning systems, security-focused tools, collaborative software, and the architecture of AI agents.

## How I approach engineering

I prefer understanding a problem before choosing a technology. That means identifying the user need, constraints, risks, and expected behaviour before settling on an implementation.

I value:

- **Clear architecture:** responsibilities should be separated and interfaces should be understandable.
- **Security and user control:** permissions, data handling, and failure behaviour should be deliberate.
- **Practical verification:** claims about software should be supported by tests or observable behaviour.
- **Documentation:** design decisions and limitations should be visible to the next person working on the project.
- **Continuous learning:** research and implementation should inform each other.

## Current interests

My current work is particularly focused on AI agent architecture, privacy-conscious software, cybersecurity, observability, and tools that make development workflows more useful.

I am open to software engineering opportunities, research collaboration, and projects where I can contribute while continuing to develop as an engineer.
